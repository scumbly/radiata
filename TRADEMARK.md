## What requires a rename

If you **distribute a modified version** of Radiata, you must:

1. **Use a different name** — one not confusingly similar to "Radiata".
2. **Remove the flower mark** — the application icon (`Assets/radiata.ico`), the About-screen flower, and the
   code-drawn flower renderings.
3. **Not present your version as the official one** — different download pages, no implication of
   endorsement.

You should (and per the license's additional terms, must) retain the "Based on Radiata" attribution
notice — crediting the origin is not the same as trading on the name.

## Why this policy exists

The name and mark are how users find the genuine, maintained, signed build. This policy follows the
same pattern as other GPL projects with held trademarks: the code is free;
the identity is not.
